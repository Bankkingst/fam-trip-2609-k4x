🎢 Day 2 · Thu 10 Sep

Chimelong Spaceship — indoor park, attached to the hotel

  • 08:00Breakfast, Sailor Buffet (hotel) — booked, show your room's order number at the cashier. Buffet runs 07:00–10:30.
  • 09:30Park entry — tickets are in the hotel package, nothing to buy. Foreign passports go through the staffed lane (15–20 min). Hotel guests may get early entry — ask at the desk.
  • 10:00Space Exploration, Cosmic World, the big aquarium tunnels. Rides for the kids, shows for everyone. Check the board at the gate for the orca show times (usually two a day, around 12:00 and 16:00) and build the day around one.
  • 12:30Lunch at the park food court.
  • 13:30Back to the room to rest — the hotel is inside the park complex. Or the rooftop infinity pool on 28F (open again from 14:00 after cleaning) — bring swimwear.
  • 16:00Back in for the zones you missed. Park closes 18:00.
  • 18:30Dinner: hotel, or Cantonese in Hengqin (Novotown / Hengqin Avenue, 10–15 min taxi).

🧳 Before you leave Bangkok

  • Passport valid 6+ months. No visa needed for Thai passports (China 30 days, Macau 30 days).
  • Fill in the China online Arrival Card (NIA 12367 app or WeChat/Alipay mini-program).
  • Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with your Thai card — it takes a day to verify.
  • Cash: CNY 1,000–2,000 for China; some MOP or HKD for Macau.
  • Turn on a VPN before you fly — LINE, Google, WhatsApp are blocked in Hengqin (Macau is fine). Or buy a roaming eSIM that covers China.
  • Rain jacket, hat, sunscreen, comfortable shoes.

Times are a plan, not a schedule — park hours and show times can change; check at the gate each morning.
